PANDAS 3 Retirement

The time has come for PANDAS 3 to retire.

PANDAS 3 relies on older products and technologies that are becoming increasingly difficult (and expensive) for the NLA to maintain and support.

Over the past few years we've progressively moved features over to PANDAS 4, which we think is well-placed to support web archiving into the future.

If you discover a critical functionality gap, or there's a PANDAS 3 feature you no longer have access to and miss, please let us know.

Thank you for your support of PANDAS 3!
